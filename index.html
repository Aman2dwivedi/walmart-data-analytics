Executive Market Report & Business Insights

Key strategic findings extracted by Aman Dwivedi from the Walmart multi-country dataset

DAX & Storytelling
1

📍 Portland Store Milestone (December)

Portland reached over 1,000 sales transactions in December, establishing itself as one of the highest-performing urban store locations in the Pacific Northwest corridor. A dedicated Power BI bookmark was created to filter directly into this high-velocity store.

2

🏆 Top 10 Product Brands Driver

The top 10 product brands (led by Hermanos, Ebony, Tell Tale, and Tri-State) generate over 25% (1/4th) of overall retail revenue and consistently exceed their individual revenue targets with profit margins above 58%.

3

⚠️ Return Rate Anomaly (+2.9%)

While current month transactions (+5.69%) and profit (+5.61%) showed strong growth, return rates rose by 2.9% (496 returns). Conditional DAX alerts highlighted specific brands like Horatio (1.25%) and Nationeel (1.18%) that require quality assurance investigations.

4

🇲🇽 Mexico Market Expansion Opportunity

Mexico recorded 72.81K transactions with a higher month-over-month profit acceleration than the US market. The business recommendation is to allocate additional logistics capacity and marketing spend to Mexico's retail hubs.
